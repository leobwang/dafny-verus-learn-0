\section*{Problem B1 (concept)}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{A.} (a) Does not verify; verification fails on the \texttt{ensures}.
\quad (b) The exit leaves only ``not \dfy{i < |s|}'': the loop never says
\dfy{i} cannot overshoot, so equality is out of reach. \\
\textbf{B.} (a) Verifies. \quad (b) Counting a natural down to the first
non-positive value lands on exactly 0, and the count itself is the
measure. \\
\textbf{C.} (a) Does not verify. \quad (b) For an odd \dfy{n}, say 3, the
values are 0, 2, 4, \dots: the step from 2 breaks \dfy{i <= n}, and \dfy{i}
never equals \dfy{n}, so the loop does not stop; Dafny reports the
termination obligation. \\
\textbf{D.} (a) Refused before verification, at \texttt{resolve}. \quad (b)
In Verus every exec loop states its own \texttt{decreases}; there is no
default. \\
\textbf{The measure Dafny uses when none is written:} the gap between the
two sides of the guard, oriented by its comparison. It is A's problem only in
appearance (A terminates) and is exactly C's problem (the gap can grow).
\end{answer}
