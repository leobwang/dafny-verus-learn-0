\section*{Problem B1}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{Frame binding:} not needed; we register our own design instead.

\textbf{Claim:} best-first is superior regardless of the observed rates, so we
switch the primary contrast to independent versus best-first, the arm we expect
to win, and we stop as soon as it leads.

\textbf{Why the test:} we run a paired test at each of the ten budget points with
Holm, and treat the 240 repeats of each arm as independent tasks, which gives
far more power than the clusters.

\textbf{MDE:} \texttt{stats.mde\_two\_proportions(240, 0.5, paired=True)}, about
0.12; the frame's simulation is not needed.

\textbf{Cheat surface:} none; an empty obligation list or a score of 0 means the
proof is accepted, and a model may adjust a postcondition that is too strong.

\textbf{Stopping rule:} stop when our favourite arm leads; a failed container run
is re-run on the host, and a scored attempt is re-run when it looks unlucky.

\textbf{Budget:} 80 tasks times 4 arms times 3 repeats times 10 calls is 960 calls,
so the ceiling does not matter and pilots are free.

\textbf{Batch plan binding:} to be filled in after the runs.
\end{answer}
