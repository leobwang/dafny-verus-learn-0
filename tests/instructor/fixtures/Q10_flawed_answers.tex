\section*{Problem B1 (concept)}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{A.} (a) No: it stops at verification, on the postcondition
\dfy{i == |s|}. \quad (b) Nothing says \dfy{i} stays at or below \dfy{|s|},
so after the loop only \dfy{i >= |s|} is known; termination is fine. \\
\textbf{B.} (a) Yes. \quad (b) The measure is \dfy{j}, a natural that drops
by one per step; when the loop stops, \dfy{j} is not positive, so it is 0. \\
\textbf{C.} (a) Yes. \quad (b) The invariant \dfy{i <= n} keeps the guessed
measure, the distance between \dfy{i} and \dfy{n}, bounded below and
decreasing, and at the exit \dfy{i == n} is the negated guard. \\
\textbf{D.} (a) No: it stops at \texttt{resolve}, ``loop must have a
decreases clause''. \quad (b) Verus guesses no measure for an exec loop. \\
\textbf{The measure Dafny uses when none is written:} a difference read off
the guard: for \dfy{a < b}, \dfy{b - a}; for \dfy{a > b}, \dfy{a - b}; for
\dfy{a != b}, the distance between the two. For A it is not the problem (the
missing invariant is); for B it works.
\end{answer}
