\section*{Problem B1 (paper card)}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{Source:} cheatsheet entry 02.3, lines 642--668 (the pinned range).

\textbf{Task shape:} A task is a specification file in one of three languages
(Dafny, Verus or Lean) with its implementation and its proof cut out and
replaced by placeholders such as \texttt{sorry} or \texttt{assume(false)}; the
model must write the missing code and proof so that the whole file checks.
Tasks are short, under 100 lines.

\textbf{Oracle:} Success is decided by the language's own checker: a run
counts as solved when Dafny, Verus or Lean accepts the filled file. The
bypass-pattern scan only flags suspicious constructs for the authors' manual
review; it does not change the verdict.

\textbf{Trust:} The specifications are taken as correct statements of intent,
even though more than half of the 10,868 tasks used were produced by an LLM
translating or autoformalizing them; the checkers and the pattern scan are
trusted as well.

\textbf{Cheat surface:} A filled file can pass while proving little. Specs
that are too weak or mistranslated let a trivial body through (the authors'
spot check of solved tasks found about 9\% too weak and 15\% badly translated;
an empty list satisfied a sort-and-deduplicate spec), and an escape hatch the
pattern scan does not know about would pass too.

\textbf{Metric:} The percentage of tasks solved, per language. The best single
model, Opus 4.1, solves 82\% of the Dafny tasks; Verus and Lean are far lower
(44\% and 27\%).

\textbf{Budget:} Nine off-the-shelf models, five turns each per task (ten on
the BigNum tasks), with checker errors as the only feedback between turns. No
token or cost figures are given.

\textbf{Variance:} Each model was run three times per task and the results come
with 95\% confidence intervals, so the gap between Dafny and Verus is well
outside the noise.

\textbf{Artifact:} The released benchmark: 12,504 specifications, 10,868 of
them kept after quality filtering, across Dafny, Verus and Lean. The card does
not say where it is hosted or under what licence.
\end{answer}
