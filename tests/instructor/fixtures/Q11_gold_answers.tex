\section*{Problem B1 (hand-calculation)}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{(a) The trace.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The conditional (rule: conditional, as a case split on the condition
  just before it). If $y > 10$, the assignment runs: by the assignment rule,
  $Q$ with $y - 10$ for $y$, that is $y - 10 < 5$. If $y \le 10$, nothing
  runs, so $Q$ itself, $y < 5$, must already hold. Together:
  $(y > 10 \wedge y - 10 < 5) \vee (y \le 10 \wedge y < 5)$.
\item The sequence: the first assignment is substituted into that formula
  (rule: assignment, $x + 3$ for $y$):
  $(x + 3 > 10 \wedge x + 3 - 10 < 5) \vee (x + 3 \le 10 \wedge x + 3 < 5)$.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Simplified, over $x$ alone:} the first disjunct is $x \ge 8 \wedge
x \le 11$ (integers: $x > 7$ is $x \ge 8$, $x < 12$ is $x \le 11$); the
second is $x \le 7 \wedge x \le 1$, that is $x \le 1$. So $x \le 1 \vee
(x \ge 8 \wedge x \le 11)$.

\textbf{(b)} $x = 10$ satisfies it: $y = 13 > 10$, then $y = 3$; $3 < 5$:
$Q$ holds. $x = 3$ does not: $y = 6$, unchanged; $6 < 5$ is false: $Q$
fails.

\textbf{(c)} $\{\dots, -1, 0, 1\} \cup \{8, 9, 10, 11\}$.
\end{answer}

\section*{Problem B2 (paper card)}\label{ans:B2}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{Source:} cheatsheet entry 02.12, lines 917--940 (the pinned range).

\textbf{Task shape:} An industrial campaign to show, function by function,
that the Rust standard library (about 34k functions, roughly 7,500 of them
unsafe) has none of a chosen set of undefined behaviours; challenges with
bounties and a review committee, plus a non-LLM harness generator for the
bulk.

\textbf{Oracle:} CI runs the tools on every PR: bounded model checkers (Kani
over CBMC, ESBMC), Flux and VeriFast; a harness passes when, for every
bit-valid input, the contract's precondition rules out the checked UB and
gives the postcondition.

\textbf{What is trusted:} Unverified intrinsic models and toolchain, the
tools themselves, and whatever contract a reviewer accepted; checks are
bounded unless said otherwise.

\textbf{Cheat surface:} The UB not on the list (aliasing, races, inline
assembly, part of provenance, termination); bounds; 622 unsafe functions
that pass without a contract and so prove little. How a weak contract would
be caught, beyond review, is not described.

\textbf{Metric:} Numbers of harnesses and functions: of 16,748 generated
harnesses, 11,970 verify; 989 functions are contract-verified (694 by hand,
295 automatically); 4,778 harnesses fail; zero new memory-safety bugs.

\textbf{Budget:} The range has no compute or cost figure; only the scale of
the effort (29 challenges, over 450 PRs, 21 or more outside contributors) and
a 3.97x compile speedup.

\textbf{Variance:} Nothing on repeated runs; tool verdicts, not samples.

\textbf{Artifact:} The verification setup in the standard library's CI; the
range names no separate release.
\end{answer}
