\section*{Problem B1 (concept)}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
$C_1$: (i)? yes \quad (ii)? yes \quad witnesses: none needed; $x \ge 0$
admits every $x$ from $0$ to $10$, and the postcondition is the trait's. \\
$C_2$: (i)? no \quad (ii)? yes \quad witnesses: $x = 0$ is admitted by the
trait and refused by $1 \le x \le 10$. \\
$C_3$: (i)? yes \quad (ii)? yes \quad witnesses: none needed; $y = x + 1$ is
more than $x$. \\
$C_4$: (i)? yes \quad (ii)? no \quad witnesses: $(2, 2)$: $0 \le 2 \le 10$
and $2 \ge 2$, but $2 > 2$ is false. \\
$C_5$: (i)? yes \quad (ii)? no \quad witnesses: $(-1, -5)$: $x \ge 0$ is
false, so $Q_I$ holds, but $-5 > -1$ is false. \\
\textbf{(c)} Condition (i) protects the call: the client can only show $P_T$
before calling, so whatever the implementation requires must already follow
from it. Condition (ii) protects what comes after: the client goes on as if
$Q_T$ held of the result, so the implementation's postcondition must give it.
\end{answer}
