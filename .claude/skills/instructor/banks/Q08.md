### K1 [connect] — a client of a frozen statement
**viva:** yes · **strand:** S6 · **tags:** `refinement`, `traits-and-interfaces`, `frozen-statements`, `requires-ensures` · **backward-edge:** Q05 (a caller knows a call only through its contract)

In Q05 a caller knew a call only through the callee's contract. Connect that to Q08's frozen statements: what does a client of an abstract module (Dafny) or of a trait (Verus) know about the implementation behind it, and how does it learn it? Read the probes: in the Dafny file, why does `TwoBeads` verify and `TwoBeadsBare` fail, when `BeadNat` makes both obviously true? Why does the same bare lemma verify against `BeadNat` itself? What does the Verus generic client use, and what would it lose if `thread`'s `ensures` were dropped?

**Target insights**
- A client of the abstract statements sees the frozen declarations and nothing else: a lemma call puts one frozen `ensures` in context at its arguments, exactly as a method call put a contract in Q05. `TwoBeads` calls the three facts it needs; `TwoBeadsBare` calls none, and the abstract `Beads`, `Thread` and `Empty` have no bodies to unfold.
- Through `import S : BeadSpec` the client is checked once for every refinement, so it may use only what every refinement promises; through `import N = BeadNat` it sees `BeadNat`'s bodies, and the bare lemma follows by unfolding. Which one a client imports is the choice between "every implementation" and "this one".
- Verus: `two_beads<N: Necklace>` uses only the trait's `ensures` (`empty` gives 0, each `thread` adds 1) and the `requires` of `thread` (below 1000), which those make true. With `thread`'s `ensures` dropped it knows nothing after a call (the chapter's P6 gap is the weak case of the same fact).
- Who checks: Dafny checks each refining lemma against the inherited statement, and the client against the abstract one; Verus checks each `impl` method against the trait's clauses, and the generic fn against the trait. The statement is the interface in both directions.

**Misconceptions & counter-moves**
- *"The client verifies because `BeadNat` is the only refinement, so Dafny uses it."* → Probe: the Dafny file; ask: which module does `BeadUser` name, and what could a second refinement define `Beads` to be?
- *"The bare lemma is just missing automation; more fuel would fix it."* → Ask: what body of `Beads` would the fuel unfold, in `BeadSpec`? Then show the second probe, where the body exists.
- *"A generic Verus client sees the impl's `open spec fn` too."* → Ask: which `impl` would `N` be in `two_beads`? Point at §1.3's last paragraph.

    fn empty() -> (r: Self)

### K2 [predict] — too weak for the client, too strong for the implementation
**viva:** yes (verifier-refereed predict) · **strand:** S6 · **tags:** `traits-and-interfaces`, `refinement`, `spec-strength`, `requires-ensures` · **backward-edge:** none

A pail holds at most 100 litres; filling adds 10 and caps at 100. The implementation is fixed; a client fills twice from at most 80 and expects 20 more. Three candidate statements for one fill, written once in a frozen declaration (Dafny: the `ensures` of `FillSpec` in `PailSpec`; Verus: the `ensures` of the trait method `fill`):

- [a] Dafny `v <= 90 ==> Fill(v) > v`; Verus `old(self).litres() <= 90 ==> final(self).litres() > old(self).litres()`
- [b] Dafny `Fill(v) == v + 10`; Verus `final(self).litres() == old(self).litres() + 10`
- [c] Dafny `v <= 90 ==> Fill(v) == v + 10`; Verus `old(self).litres() <= 90 ==> final(self).litres() == old(self).litres() + 10`

            self.ml = 100;

}

} // verus!

### K3 [transfer] — which instrument sees which move
**viva:** yes · **strand:** S6 · **tags:** `frozen-statements`, `witnesses`, `vacuity`, `spec-strength`, `contract-allowlist` · **backward-edge:** Q06 (a leaf that is proved and never usable; the witness call)

Transfer the flagship's discipline to a fresh operation, `Thrice` (three times its argument). For each of three moves, say which of the flagship's instruments catches it (the gate with zero holes, `specfreeze --against` your snapshot, `vaudit`, your witnesses, or only a reader), and when (before the contract run or after): (1) you commit a statement that is too weak, `ensures r >= x`; (2) you commit a vacuous one, `requires x > x`; (3) you commit the right statement, and the contractor delivers the weak one with a body that meets it. Read the probes. Then say what card 02.1's agent did that is move (3), and which line of the flagship contract is there for it.

**Target insights**
- (1) Your witness catches it, before the run, but only because the witness asserts what you *meant* (`r == 6`): a witness that asserts only what the statement gives passes. A delivery under the weak statement is green and audit-clean (`r := x` meets `r >= x`); the gate cannot see that the statement is weak.
- (2) Your witness catches it, before the run: the call's precondition fails. Delivered, the vacuous statement is proved by any body (`r := 0`): the gate is green and `vaudit` reports nothing, since nothing is assumed (Q06's useless leaf).
- (3) `specfreeze --against` your snapshot catches it, after the run: `freeze-changed`, naming the method. The gate, again, is green.
- So the instruments split the work: witnesses check the statement against your intent before anyone proves it, the freeze checks the delivery against the statement, the gate and the audit check the proof. Card 02.1's agent silently weakened a statement (a fuel bound removed): the freeze line is for that, and it works only against a snapshot you committed first.

**Misconceptions & counter-moves**
- *"A green gate means the statement says what I wanted."* → Probe: the gate on the weak delivery; ask: which line of the output mentions three?
- *"The audit would flag a vacuous statement."* → Probe: `vaudit` on it; ask: what is assumed there?
- *"Witnesses only need to call each operation."* → Ask: what does a call with no assertion rule out? Then compare the two witness probes.

**viva:** yes · **strand:** S8 · **tags:** `verified-metatheory`, `vacuity`, `wp-vc` · **backward-edge:** none
