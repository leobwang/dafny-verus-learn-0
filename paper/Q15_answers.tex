\textbf{Feedback signals} (each signal S1 receives after a sample, named, and

\textbf{Metric and estimator} (the oracle verdict against the hidden reference;

the paired estimator of S1 $-$ S0): \dots

\textbf{Arms and policies} (S0, S1, V and the Claude arm, each with its policy
file, render mode and prompt hash; the Claude subset's seed and its
stratification): \dots

\textbf{Tasks and strata} (the local stratum's file and its sha256, the public
strata's sources and commits, the validation files' sha256, the draw seeds):
\dots

\textbf{Budget arithmetic} (calls = tasks $\times$ attempts $\times$ rounds
$\times$ arms $\times$ repeats, per arm, plus the sentinel calls; the runs; the
throughput assumption and its source; against the registry's budget): \dots

\textbf{Generation settings} (the values the Ollama gate pins for every Qwen
call; the seed list per arm, pairwise disjoint; for Claude the pinned argv):
\dots

\textbf{Cost per arm} (tokens in and out, GPU-seconds and wall clock per arm,
against the ceiling; the truncation rate per arm as a sensitivity line; the
fallback if the per-arm totals exceed the ceiling): \dots

\textbf{MDE} (computed with \texttt{fvh.stats.mde\_two\_proportions}: $n$,
baseline, pairing, $\alpha$, the call and its value): \dots

\textbf{Seeds and repeats} (Qwen: the per-request seeds; Claude: repeated
runs): \dots

\textbf{Oracles' version} (the sha256 of \texttt{harness/q/fvh\_q/oracles.py}
at the run, and the command that computes it): \dots

\textbf{Cheat surface and gate} (the instruments every claimed success passes,
where \texttt{implication} and \texttt{classify} run, \texttt{UNKNOWN} and
\texttt{UNDECIDED} handling): \dots

\textbf{Exclusions} (\texttt{infra-failed} re-issue; \texttt{truncated:<cause>}
as failures, with the sensitivity line; a killed oracle run): \dots

\textbf{Stopping rule}: \dots

\textbf{Prediction intervals} (80\%, for each claim's estimate, scored after
the run): \dots

\textbf{Contamination} (the local stratum's authorship dates; the card
contamination audit: novelty review and perturbation probes; the public sets
flagged as possibly seen): \dots

\textbf{Disclosure} (a small-scale replication with model substitution; Claude as
grader and subject; one machine, one solver pin): \dots

\textbf{Batch plan} (the plan file \texttt{python -m fvh run Q15 -{}-plan}
reads: its path, and its sha256 on a line of its own as \texttt{plan-sha256:}
followed by the 64 hex digits \texttt{sha256sum} prints for the file;
\texttt{fvh run} opens a lab batch only on the plan this line names;
separately executed pilot or subset plans need their own binding and a new
gradebook row after re-grading the changed pre-registration, with the
registered method held fixed): \dots
\end{answer}
