\begin{problem}[proof]\label{prob:B2}
The intended operation returns whether a regex occurs in any substring,
including an empty one. Compare these postconditions for a returned Boolean $b$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $b\Leftrightarrow\mathsf{Occurs}(r,s)$;
\item $b\Leftrightarrow\mathsf{Matches}(r,s)$;
\item $b\Rightarrow\mathsf{Occurs}(r,s)$;
\item $b\Leftrightarrow\exists i:0\leq i\leq |s|.\ \mathsf{Matches}(r,s[i..])$.
\end{enumerate}
Use $r=\mathsf{Chr}('m')$ and $s=\texttt{mn}$, a different pair from the
predictions. For each contract give every permitted Boolean and the substring
or slice facts that establish it. Then classify its general relationship to
the intended operation: faithful, too weak or a different semantic question.
A single input's agreement is not a universal equivalence argument.
\end{problem}

\end{problem}
