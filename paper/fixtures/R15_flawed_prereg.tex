\section*{Problem B6 (pre-registration)}\label{ans:B6}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{Claim.} If $\kappa$ is at least 0.6 the two oracles are validated:
$\kappa$ is the share of specifications both oracles judge correctly, so
$\kappa = 0.6$ means each oracle is right about 60\% of the time.

\textbf{Metric and estimator.} Unit: one specification (card $\times$ variant
$\times$ language), 192 units. \texttt{spectest} says faithful when the
specification is sound on every pair and kills every decided mutant;
\texttt{roundtrip} says faithful when its verdict is equivalent; an unparsed or
unknown roundtrip reply counts as equivalent. Cohen's $\kappa$ over the 192
units. The confusion matrices against the hidden verdicts and the
verified-but-unfaithful rates, Dafny against Verus, are descriptive.

\textbf{Arms, tasks and the Claude subset.} The 24 cards (10 mine, 14 the
author's) with four variants in both languages; a Qwen \texttt{roundtrip} arm,
a Qwen delivery arm and a Claude delivery arm on 20 cards, 10 per language,
drawn with seed 4417 and stratified by language. To
keep the four rounds productive, after each round the loop also tells the model
whether the hidden verdict for its specification is faithful (read from
\texttt{Hidden/labs/R15/} by the runner).

\textbf{Budget arithmetic.} Qwen: 24 $\times$ 4 $\times$ 8 = 768 delivery
calls and 24 $\times$ 4 $\times$ 2 = 192 roundtrip calls, 960 in all, exactly
the registry; $\kappa$ is computed over all 192 units (24 $\times$ 4 $\times$ 2
languages). Claude: 20 $\times$ 4 = 80 calls; sentinel calls are not model work
and are not counted.

\textbf{Generation settings.} Thinking off, \texttt{num\_predict} 768,
\texttt{num\_ctx} 16384, temperature 0.7 for every Qwen call; seeds one per
run, 15001--15192 for \texttt{roundtrip} and 15501--15692 for deliveries.

\textbf{Cost per arm.} Logged per call: tokens in and out, GPU-seconds, wall
clock.

\textbf{GPU-hours against the ceiling.} 960 $\times$ 7.3 s $\approx$ 1.9
GPU-h, under the 3-hour ceiling, so no fallback is needed.

\textbf{Truncation rate.} Not needed at \texttt{num\_predict} 768.

\textbf{Precision.} The 192 units are independent, so the interval is the
usual $\kappa$ standard error with $n = 192$; the MDE from
\texttt{mde\_two\_proportions(192, 0.5)} is about 0.14.

\textbf{Cheat surface and gate.} A delivery could change the statement, add an
assumption or a lever; every verified delivery is re-judged by the gate, the
audit and the freeze in the verification container.

\textbf{Missing results and exclusions.} An apparatus failure is re-run once
with the same seed, then excluded and counted.

\textbf{\texttt{firsttry} intervals.} $\kappa$ in [0.5, 0.9]; verified-but-unfaithful
rate Dafny [0.1, 0.4], Verus [0.1, 0.4].

\textbf{Stopping rule.} We look at $\kappa$ after every 48 units and stop once
its interval excludes 0.4.

\textbf{Fallback.} None.

\textbf{Contamination.} All 24 cards are new, so contamination is impossible
and no perturbation probe or card audit is needed.

\textbf{Disclosure.} Model ids and digests, tool versions, prompt hashes and the
oracles' sha256 are recorded. Claude is not a
subject in Lab $\beta$ because the oracles use Qwen. The Claude arm uses seeds
1--20.

\textbf{Batch plan binding.} \texttt{plan-sha256:} to be filled in after the
batch.
\end{answer}
