\section*{Problem B1}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{Task shape:} An independent Lean4 kernel written in Lean replays compiled declarations through addDecl, compared with lean4checker. The specification connects abstract de Bruijn terms, a typing/definitional-equality judgment, a translation relation and a one-sided monadic Hoare logic.

\textbf{Oracle:} Executable replay checks declarations. Formal metatheory is partial: weakening, substitution and regularity are proved; unique typing, definitional inversion and strengthening remain open. Replay success is distinct from correctness of the executable kernel relative to the intended type theory.

\textbf{Trust:} The implementation mirrors the C++ algorithm and shares Expr and the compiler, leaving correlated errors possible. Inductive metatheory is mostly missing; the proofs depend on open conjectures and the unsound looseBVarRange\_eq axiom. There is no complete consistency proof for the new Lean4 features.

\textbf{Cheat surface:} An agent may receive reward for a weak or unfaithful statement or bypass the kernel with a metaprogram. Independent replay can expose some implementation discrepancies without determining faithfulness. A proof about altered rules can still be true. Partial-correctness evidence is not a termination or total-correctness guarantee.

\textbf{Budget:} Four recursion entry points break mutual recursion with fuel1000 nested calls. Nontermination of the underlying type theory makes a termination argument unavailable in general. The excerpt supplies no common token, dollar, sampling or actor-turn budget.

\textbf{Variance:} The excerpt reports one hardware setup and single-threaded package timings, without a repeated-run distribution or confidence interval. Three packages are not three independent repeated trials of one experiment. Unsupported reduceBool and missing metatheory restrict generalization.

\section*{Problem B2}\label{ans:B2}
\begin{answer}
Checker agreement relates its returned Boolean exactly to fixed A4 validity.
Soundness derives matching from validity. Direct completeness constructs a valid
derivation from matching; Verus states the representable word-length domain.
Producer rejection completeness rules out None on a matching word. Always-None
satisfies a conditional Some-valid promise and violates the rejection promise.
A changed Star rule may accept a valid first chunk and ignore an invalid suffix.
Renaming letters consistently can preserve this wrong relation; that transport
proof does not prove equality to A4. Compare pure model definitions independently
against arbitrary canonical subjects and preserve the original source snapshot.

From $i<n$ and $n\leq\mathit{usize::MAX}$ obtain $i+1\leq n$ and no overflow.
For current residual $q$ and consumed prefix $p$, require for every suffix $t$
that $q$ matches $t$ iff the original regex matches $p+t$. Derivative correctness
advances this relation. Nullability observes the just-consumed prefix. The trace
length is $|p|$, and each trace position records its corresponding nonempty
prefix; termination uses remaining input. Empty input has an empty trace but
still requires the original regex's nullable final answer.

Runtime derivations are ordinary data read by the compiled checker. Dafny ghost
records and Verus tracked evidence are erased proof data. A validity premise on
a tracked wrapper remains an explicit hypothesis: constructing an arbitrary
wrapper does not establish validity. Verus moves tracked evidence in proof mode;
it is not a runtime authority or a PointsTo cell permission. The mutable tracked
output slot avoids a non-void proof return in the canonical empty-body gate.
The checkpoint counts consumed character leaves and constructs repeated-character
certificates in a fresh loop; it does not call the general soundness or search
solutions. Dafny's printed induction-disabled lever makes the designated empty
proofs fail although max-auto can prove them.

The Vec fallback bounds targets and specifies the exact changed edge and
unchanged nodes. Rust borrowing controls executable access; these results do not
implement or prove the skipped PointsTo permission arena. Full native gates,
assumption audits, immutable original snapshots and independent rule-model
checks answer different questions. Finite compiled observations can falsify
particular defects and test actual emitted code; they are not universal
metatheory or evidence of deferred live actor sessions.
\end{answer}
