\section*{Problem B1}\label{ans:B1}
\begin{answer}
This is the author's method exemplar inside the course-fixed frame, not a graded
registration, a live result or a run authorization: the learner writes, binds and
has graded their own registration before any run.

\textbf{Frame binding.} \texttt{freeze-sha256: 763a8f267702de09690e6411e95d5ffe57ad1e512445b751b804aea987e1530d}. The frame fixes the
contrast, the estimand $\theta$, the test, the budget axis, the aggregation, the
missing-result rule, the resource accounting, the corpus, the composition and
$K = 80$; this registration cites them by name and changes none of them.

\textbf{Claim, expected direction and refutation.} The fixed contrast asks
whether best-first search finds a verified hint set earlier, in node calls, when
its feedback names the failing obligation and its span rather than handing the
model the whole wrapper document. I expect $\theta$ near zero, slightly negative:
the localized list is shorter, but the raw document also carries the context
around the failing site, and the course's three five-cluster pilots all ran the
other way ($\theta = -0.04$, $-0.03$, $-0.10$, never a test). An interval wholly
above zero refutes that expectation in favour of localization, one wholly below
zero confirms that raw feedback is better here, and an interval spanning zero
leaves the direction unresolved, which is a result, not a failure of the study.

\textbf{Prediction interval for theta.} 80\% prediction interval
$[-0.10, 0.04]$ AUC points, written before any data: centred a little below
zero, as the pilots were, and wider than the reproduced MDE below, because five
pilot clusters say little about eighty.

\textbf{Why the fixed test.} The cluster (a lemma family's Dafny and Verus forms,
or one public task) is the unit because forms of one lemma share their
difficulty, and repeats and budget points of one run are not independent draws;
averaging inside the cluster first and then testing the 80 cluster differences
respects that. One paired sign-flip test on those differences replaces a test at
each budget point: the estimand already integrates the ten budget points into one
area per run, so there is one hypothesis and no multiplicity correction; the
sign-flip test needs no normality of the differences. The whole-cluster bootstrap
interval reports the size of $\theta$, not only its sign. One test cannot locate
where the four regimes' curves cross: crossings are described from the bands and
never tested.

\textbf{MDE reproduction.} With the stratum's confirmatory clusters (one four-task, 28 two-task and 51 one-task clusters)
and the frame's \texttt{power\_sim\_cell} arguments:
\begin{verbatim}
from fvh import stats
for base, tau in ((0.35, 0.5), (0.35, 1.0), (0.60, 0.5), (0.60, 1.0)):
    print(base, tau, stats.mde_paired_clusters_sim(
        {4: 1, 2: 28, 1: 51}, 3, base, 0.15, tau, n_sims=1000, n_delta=30000, seed=20261017))
\end{verbatim}
prints 0.0900, 0.1016, 0.0818, 0.0973, the frame's four values. The design cannot detect
differences much below about 0.08 AUC points; effect heterogeneity across
clusters and the number of clusters set that bound, and a fourth repeat would move
it little, because the arms already share every cluster's and task's difficulty.

\textbf{Cheat surface and gates.} A delivery could change a statement, a model or a
lever (the lab gate's lever check and the source-frame guard refuse it); a reply
could be read as success from an empty obligation list or an unknown score (only
the full gate's clean verified result is success, re-judged in the container); a
reply could escape a site with a statement or a trust word (the token grammar and
the frame guard refuse it, a failed node); a container failure could tempt a host
fallback (none exists: the run is an apparatus failure, re-run once); a scored
attempt could be re-run for a better outcome (the ledger refuses it).

\textbf{Stopping rule.} A run stops at its first verified candidate, after ten node
calls, or when its fixed frontier is exhausted. A batch that reaches R12's 80
GPU-hour ceiling pauses between attempts and resumes later; no scored attempt is
re-run, and an interrupted attempt is an apparatus failure, re-run once with
the same seeds (the frame's \texttt{missing\_rule}). The study stops when
every planned attempt of the frame's fixed $N$ is settled, never at an arm's lead.

\textbf{Seeds and plans.} Confirmatory plan from \texttt{tools/R12/plan.py --seed
20261101 --roles confirmatory}; descriptive plan from the same call with
\texttt{--roles descriptive}; the per-call seeds derive from those base seeds and
each attempt id.

\textbf{Budget, cost and truncation sensitivity.} Confirmatory: 111 tasks
$\times$ 2 arms $\times$ 3 repeats $\times$ 10 = 6{,}660 calls at most;
descriptive: 34 tasks $\times$ 2 $\times$ 3 $\times$ 10 = 2{,}040;
together 8{,}700 of R12's 10,700 (the Claude subset's 100 calls and 10
sentinels are counted apart). At about 72 GPU-seconds per call (the measured cost
on this corpus) the ceiling case would be 174 GPU-hours, more than R12's 80; but
a run stops at its first success, and the pilots spent a quarter to a third of
their planned calls, which puts the expected cost near 42 to 63 GPU-hours (the
frame's \texttt{cost} field). If the batch
reaches 80 GPU-hours it pauses between attempts and the rest stay planned for a
later resume (the stopping rule). The confirmatory plan runs its two arms in
adjacent pairs (\texttt{interleave}), so a pause leaves whole pairs behind it, and
$\theta$ uses only clusters whose runs are settled in both arms (the frame's
\texttt{aggregation}); the confirmatory plan runs before the descriptive one.
Sensitivity: the share of calls cut at the output cap and the feedback cap's
truncation rate, per arm, with $\theta$ recomputed on the cumulative prompt-token
axis.

\textbf{Descriptive analyses.} The four regimes' success curves with
whole-cluster bootstrap bands (\texttt{fvh.stats.cluster\_bootstrap\_ci}), read as
pointwise bands; crossings read off the bands and never tested; the regex and
public strata and the Dafny and Verus views; the Claude subset's ten runs.

\textbf{Contamination and disclosure.} The regex forms are course-derived
(written after the pinned model's release, previously seen by me); the public
forms are possibly seen by the model; the strata stay apart in every table; the
sealed R13/R14 clusters are outside every plan. One model, one hardware and solver
pin, these prompts and $B = 10$ bound the claim; Claude is both a subject and the
grader; only the token-axis line separates localization from a shorter prompt.

\textbf{Batch plan binding.} \texttt{plan-sha256:} c2ec02fb4f873b85f62de30b3fdd3cfd520ec40ae7020f64db2cbadf52559f1c (the confirmatory plan;
the descriptive plan is registered and graded on its own).
\end{answer}
\section*{Problem B2}\label{ans:B2}
\begin{answer}
No live result is supplied: the confirmatory and descriptive runs are the
learner's, and this exemplar invents no outcome. A valid report has this shape.

\textbf{Reproduce.} One command, \texttt{fvh report R12 --labalpha --rejudged
FILE}, regenerates every number from the frozen batch logs; the report names the
\texttt{freeze-sha256} and the \texttt{plan-sha256} the runs were made on.

\textbf{Theta, its interval, the test and the sensitivity line.} $\hat\theta$ is
the mean over the 80 clusters of the per-cluster AUC difference (localized minus
raw best-first); the whole-cluster bootstrap 95\% interval and the paired
sign-flip $p$ are reported once each, together with the registered prediction
interval and whether $\hat\theta$ fell inside it. The frame's worst-case line
(excluded runs scored 0 in the localized arm and 1 in the raw arm) and the
cumulative prompt-token axis line recompute $\theta$ and say whether the sign
survives.

\textbf{Curves, bands and strata.} The four regimes' success curves over the ten
node calls with pointwise whole-cluster bands; the regex and public strata and the
Dafny and Verus views stay apart; any crossing is described from the bands and is
not a tested claim.

\textbf{Re-judgement, provenance and transcript audit.} Every success is re-judged
in the pinned container under the original task, frame and model bindings; the
audit, frame-guard, statement-freeze and lever evidence travel with the logs; 20
transcript moments are audited, failed nodes, empty localization, unknown scores,
source or provenance attacks and stopped requests among them.

\textbf{Costs, truncation, deviations and prediction intervals.} GPU-hours used
against the 80-hour ceiling, pauses and resumes, the share of calls cut at the
output cap and the feedback cap's truncation rate per arm; every deviation from the
registration is listed; unrun or deferred attempt ids are kept as unrun, never
imputed; the descriptive Claude subset stays descriptive.

\textbf{Contamination and disclosure.} Course-derived regex forms and possibly
seen public forms are reported apart; one model, one hardware and solver pin and
$B = 10$ bound the conclusion; Claude is both a subject and the grader.
Mock, Scripted and Replay fixtures validate software behaviour only.
\end{answer}
\section*{Problem B3}\label{ans:B3}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{Task shape:} Coq proof synthesis retains checked prefixes and recursively repairs deepest broken subproofs. GPT-4-0613 is tested on four random100-theorem subsets, with other models also studied; there is no fine-tuning.

\textbf{Oracle:} Coq checks retained fragments and supplies the subgoal structure. CoqHammer is tried before fresh samples. The fail-safe admit marker records an in-progress goal; it is not an accepted completed proof.

\textbf{Trust:} The checker, original theorem and imported assumptions remain trusted, along with CoqHammer's checking route. Soundness of checking does not establish that a theorem faithfully encodes its intended task.

\textbf{Cheat surface:} A missing goal must not be rewarded as solved. Oracle lemma/decomposition hints are derived from human proofs and must be disclosed. Our lab never introduces admit as an allowed hint or imports the paper's repair marker as an acceptance rule.

\textbf{Metric:} Cobblestone solves48/38/44/43percent on CoqGym100, Wigderson100, PnVRocqLib100 and BB5100. PALM's token-matched52/30/35/41shows complementarity. A union63/48/59/61is not one system's rate. Added value counts new solved theorems relative to another set.

\textbf{Budget:} Each invocation first tries hammer and then four whole-proof samples from context/CoT variants; depth at most5, at most5invocations and20samples. Mean1.25USD and14.7minutes applies to the two named subsets. These are not this lab's10nodes or a matched GPU budget.

\textbf{Variance:} The excerpt gives small random subsets and no variance report. It cannot support a confidence interval, zero variance or a general all-project effect. PALM can win on one subset while union gains remain real.

\textbf{Artifact:} Kasibatla et al., ICSE2026, arXiv2410.19940. Pin the local excerpt and disclose Coq/GPT-4-era8K context and oracle sources. The excerpt does not pin a replication commit or provide this course's native hint-site environment.

\end{answer}
\section*{Problem B4}\label{ans:B4}
\begin{answer}
\textbf{Task shape:} Backward proving in Metamath, Lean3/Mathlib miniF2F and160Equations Identities with a440M--600M encoder-decoder policy/critic, supervised pretraining followed by online training.

\textbf{Oracle:} Tactics produce AND-hyperedges: each chosen tactic requires every child goal. Prover-checked solved/invalid leaves get1/0; unsolved leaves use a learned critic. A critic score is guidance, not proof acceptance.

\textbf{Trust:} Trust the actual checker and formal statement, not the policy or product of estimated child values. The product uses an independence assumption. A NumPy floating-point checker was exploited and a miniF2F statement had truncated-subtraction misformalization.

\textbf{Cheat surface:} Online learners can exploit an unsound checker or an incorrect task statement. Rewarding a critic value without final checking would create another bypass. Our source frame and original task binding address a narrower surface and do not prove checker soundness.

\textbf{Metric:} Metamath test pass@32is72.4; miniF2F-test pass@64is40.6or41.0in the named compute settings; Equations cumulative91.3is a different denominator. Cumulative transductive headline rates must not be merged with a fixed pass@k comparison.

\textbf{Budget:} Large-scale online training and search, for example1620A100-days for one7-day miniF2F setting; weights refresh every5minutes and hyperparameters vary per attempt. Training cost and generation budgets differ from this lab's fixed ten-node comparisons.

\textbf{Variance:} The excerpt supplies several benchmarks/settings and ablations but no repeated-run confidence interval for a common fixed experiment. Large compute, transductive cumulative reporting, Lean3 and small models limit transfer.

\textbf{Artifact:} Lample et al., NeurIPS2022, arXiv2205.11491, Evariste. Local lines429--456are pinned. The excerpt alone does not identify this lab's replication commit or promise that best-first native annotation search reproduces AND-hypertree learning.

\end{answer}
