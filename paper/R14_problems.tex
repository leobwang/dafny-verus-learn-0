\section*{Instructions}
Commit all five predictions before reading. Allow 30 minutes: 15 for each
problem. Card 02.5 describes a published agent harness; the tools you used in
R14 (\texttt{trigscan}, \texttt{vcheck --smt-log}) are hand-built readers of
what a solver was given, not that harness. Keep the paper's claims and the
course's tools apart.

\begin{problem}[statistics]\label{prob:B2}
\textbf{What a CV sweep licenses.} Take your ten-seed sweep of section 6 of the
chapter (one target, seeds 1 to 10, \texttt{scripts/vcheck --json --measures
--seed N}). (a) Report every run: seed, verdict, resource count and wall time,
including runs that ran out of resources, and the settings that were held
fixed (toolchain pins, resource limit, target file digest, machine). (b)
Compute the coefficient of variation of the resource count over the runs that
verified, and a 95\,\% bootstrap interval for its mean
(\texttt{fvh.stats.bootstrap\_ci}); say why runs that ran out are not averaged
in, and how you report them instead. (c) Say what the sweep licenses and what
it does not, in particular about another seed range, another Z3 version and
another machine; and whether three agreeing seeds (the done rule) and your ten
runs answer the same question. (d) Name one comparison the sweep could support
if, and only if, you had fixed it before running.
\end{problem}
